## Design Context

### Users
Action photographers culling a shoot right after it: thousands of frames fired in long bursts (one long shutter press). They sit at a laptop, want to get through every burst fast, pick the one or few sharp frames worth keeping, and archive the rest. Power users who learn the keys (Space, A, arrows, ⌘Z) and scrub a lot.

### Brand Personality
Quick, precise, calm. A focused tool, not a showcase. The photo is the hero; the UI stays out of the way and confirms actions crisply.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark, low-glare surf palette: deep ocean-navy neutrals (#0a1018, #111a25, #1b2736), muted blue-grey text #93a3b5, sea-blue #1b3350 for selected states, and a single sunlit-yellow accent #f7c948 used sparingly for focus, keep and undo (yellow details, never yellow fills behind text on dark tints). Derived from the "Focus wave" Figma direction (file Burst-bud), re-themed for surfing. The "focus wave": the active burst as a large crest with frame slivers, neighbours as small crests, the shoot as an overview bar.

### Motion
Quick and precise: 120–250 ms, ease-out-quint/expo, transform + opacity only. Scrubbing is always instant (never animated). One signature moment: archiving a burst. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

### Design Principles
1. The photo gets the space; chrome is one row.
2. Every action is instant and undoable; confirm only what loses progress.
3. Motion explains where things went (direction of travel, what got archived), never decorates.
4. Accent is rare: focus, keep, undo.
